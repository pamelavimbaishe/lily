The information collapse that costs women years.

In our communities, recurring pelvic agony is minimized as "bad cramps." By the time a patient secures a ten-minute consultation, months of crucial cyclical patterns, bleeding volumes, and failed analgesic attempts are compressed into fragmented recollection.

Doctors don't fail to treat because medicine lacks answers. They fail because they cannot act on what patients cannot cleanly structure in under ten minutes.

MODULE 01

App & PWA Journaling
